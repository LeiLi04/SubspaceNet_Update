A FAST DOA TRACKING ALGORITHM BASED ON THE 
EXTENDED KALMAN FILTER 
DONGKEON KONG and JOOHWAN CHUN 
Scientific Computing Laboratory, Dept. of EECS, KAIST, 373-1 Kusong-dong, Yusong-gu, Taejon, 305-701, 
South Korea, chun@sclab.kaist.ac.kr 
Abstract. Many direction of arrival (DOA) tracking algorithms suffer heavy computational complexity 
due to their dependence on the sample covariance matrix. We propose a new DOA tracking algorithm that 
uses the extended Kalman filter (EKF). Because the proposed algorithm is based on a single snapshot, it 
requires low computational complexity. Our simulation illustrates that the proposed algorithm performs 
better than other methods 
Key Words: DOA tracking, extended Kalman filter, Antenna array

1. INTRODUCTION 
There are many tracking algorithms using a 
sensor array [?, ?I. 
Most of the algorithms are 
based on the covariance made with a block of 
measurement snapshots obtained from a sensor 
array. 
These algorithms suffer two significant 
drawbacks. The one is that large errors could 
occur due to the assumption that the trajectory of 
the DOA is fixed or linear function of time within 
the block. The other is that the computational 
complexity of them is very large due to the need 
of processing a block of measurements at each 
sampling time. 
In this paper, we propose a new DOA track- 
ing algorithm that overcomes these shortcomings 
using an EKF-based algorithm. The EKF is the 
most widely used methods for recursive nonlinear 
estimation [?I. 
Because it depends on a single 
snapshot rather than the sample covariance, the 
required computation is significantly low and the 
adaptation to moving targets is faster than other 
ones. 
In section 2, the data model is presented and 
one of the previous algorithms in [?I is explained. 
The proposed algorithm is explained and its 
computational complexity is given in section 3. 
In section 4, we study the performance of the 
proposed algorithm through simulation. Finally 
conclusions are made in section 5.

5. CONCLUSIONS 
We present a new DOA tracking algorithm 
based on the EKF using a single snapshot. The 
performance of the proposed algorithm has been 
examined through computer simulation and 
analysis. 
It has been shown that the proposed algorithm 
has much less computational complexity in com- 
parison with the previous algorithm and has good 
performance even when targets are moving fast. 
The initialization of the EKF may be done through 
a conventional DOA estimation method such as 
MUSIC. The accurate resolution of the crossing 
problem requires further works.
