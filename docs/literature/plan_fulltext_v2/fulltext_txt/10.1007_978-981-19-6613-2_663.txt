Robust Underwater Direction-of-Arrival
Tracking with Uncertain Measurement Noise
Boxuan Zhang1, Guangen Gao2, Xiaokun Ding2, and Xianghao Hou1(B)
1 School of Marine Science and Technology, Northwestern Polytechnical University,
Xi’an 710072, Shaanxi, China
houxianghao1990@nwpu.edu.cn
2 AVIC Xi’an Flight Automatic Control Research Institute, Xi’an, China
Abstract. A robust underwater direction-of-arrival (DOA) tracking technique
is presented by the paper. Firstly, considering the port and starboard ambiguity
problem, the measurement model is founded as a non-linear sound pressure model
based on a uniform circular array. Then, considering the unknown underwater
environment can sometimes cause uncertain measurement noise, a robust DOA
tracking technique based on variational Bayesian (VB) and extended Kalman ﬁlter
(EKF) method is proposed. By utilizing proposed VB-EKF to estimate uncertain
measurement noise online, DOA tracking technique can achieve a reliable tracking
accuracy. Finally, the proposed VB-EKF is tested by simulations to verify its
robustness and tracking performance.
Keywords: DOA tracking · Variational Bayesian · Extended Kalman ﬁlter
1
Introduction
Bearing angle estimation and tracking are important research areas in the ﬁeld of
aerospace engineering, remote sensing and tracking underwater target. For underwa-
ter target tracking scenario, passive bearing angle estimation can not only supply angle
informationof interestedtarget, but alsolocalizetarget withtwoor moredifferent bearing
angle information [1]. Usually, the bearing angle estimation techniques can be divided
by two categories, namely the DOA estimation and the DOA tracking. The former one
unusually assumes the target remains stationary or make very slow maneuvers. How-
ever, in many underwater target tracking scenes, the target always performs a typical
moving mode (i.e., constant-velocity (CV) moving, constant-acceleration moving, etc.)
that the DOA estimation technique can have poor estimation results since it ignores
the target’s kinematic and dynamic information [1]. Under this circumstance, the DOA
tracking technique which considers both of the target’s kinematics and dynamics and
the measurement model can achieve more accurate estimation results if the target is
maneuvering.
Considering the superior characteristics of the DOA tracking. Many researchers have
proposed a number of nonlinear tracking algorithms to realize the DOA tracking in the
scenario of underwater target bearing angle estimation. Kong and Chun [1] proposed

a novel extended Kalman ﬁlter (EKF) based DOA tracking method dealing with the
nonlinear measurement model, which linearize the nonlinear model by applying ﬁrst-
order Taylor series expansion, i.e., Jacobian matrix, of measurement model. Depend on
similar principles that linearize nonlinear models, high-precision tracking techniques
using unscented Kalman ﬁlter [2] and cubature Kalman ﬁlter (CKF) [3, 4] are proposed.
Unlike the above methods that approximately linearize nonlinear models, the tracking
method based on particle ﬁlter (PF) [5] can theoretically realize accurate tracking in
any tracking models with any distributions of stochastic noise by generating and trans-
forming a large number of arbitrary particles. However, the PF-based tracking method
has signiﬁcant computational complexity due to its large number of particles, especially
when tracking system is nonlinear. Then, real applications are very few based on the PF
tracking method.
Also, taking account unknown underwater environment can sometimes lead to uncer-
tainmeasurementnoise,theDOAtrackingtechniqueneedstoberobusttoobtainaccurate
bearing angle estimations. The state-of-art robust tracking techniques can be divided to
two categories, namely the inspired technique (i.e., Sage-Husa online noise estimator and
innovation adaptive estimator) [6] and the Bayesian technique (i.e., variational Bayesian
estimator) [7, 8]. The former one depends on a large period of good measurements and
estimate the uncertain measurement covariance matrix by the historical good estima-
tions. However, this kind of inspired online noise estimator can hardly guarantee its
convergence and the period of good estimations is usually determined by experience.
Considering the drawbacks of the inspired techniques, the VB technique is much more
superior for it has the analytical description of the posterior probability function (PDF)
of the uncertain measurement noise. Also, by adopting the VB estimation technique,
both of the uncertain measurement noise and the current state can be estimated in the
same time [7, 8]. As a result, the VB based online noise estimating techniques are more
and more popular among the researchers to realize the robust target tracking missions.
Inspired by the VB technique and focused on the robust underwater target tracking
mission, this paper proposed a VB-EKF to achieve robust and accurate DOA tracking
of an underwater target with uncertain measurement noise. Firstly, considering a wide
range in a long-time tracking scenario, the uniform circular array (UCA) is used since
it is free from the port and starboard ambiguity problem [9]. Then, the VB-EKF is
derived based on the high nonlinear measurement model and the uncertain measurement
noise. Finally, the proposed VB-EKF is veriﬁed by the MATLAB simulations and the
robustness and accuracy are proved.

5
Conclusion
A robust underwater target DOA tracking algorithm inspired of VB technique is pro-
posed. By modeling measurement model of the raw measurement data of a UCA and
considering the uncertain measurement noise, the proposed VB-EKF can make reliable
estimations about the target’s bearing angle. The simulation results proved the proposed
VB-EKF has superior tracking performance compared to the CBF and EKF. Further
work will focus on the computational effective variations of the robust DOA tracking
based on VB and their experiment veriﬁcation.
