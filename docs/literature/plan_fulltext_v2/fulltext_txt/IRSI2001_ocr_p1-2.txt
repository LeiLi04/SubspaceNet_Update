ABSTRACT

Tracking of multiple targets by using only the bearing information is an important
problem in passive sensing scenarios such as passive radar or infrared seeker
applications. In this paper, a.new algorithm is presented to adaptively estimate the
AOAs of multiple moving targets when Linear Equispace Array (LES) is’ used for making
the measurements. This algorithm is based on an extension of Differential MUSIC
method. It can also discriminate sources and their multi-paths. The results obtained by
this algorithm are then applied as input to a Non - Linear State Linear Measurements
Unscented Kalman filter. The Unscented Kalman filter also adapts to maneuver. This
algorithm gives 50percent reduction in the computation and memory requirements over
unscented filter based tracker. Simulation results show the good performance of the
filter over Unscented filter based tracker.

1. INTRODUCTION

Direction of Arrival (DOA) estimation in a passive radar scenario has attracted
immense interest among researchers in the last two decades. Among the high
resolution DOA estimation methods proposed, those based on the estimation of noise
subspace / signal subspace vectors, in order to finally estimate the DOAs of the sources
become very popular owing to their superior resolution capabilities and better bias
performances. Notable among the Eigen structure methods is the MUSIC algorithm
proposed by Schmidt [1]. This algorithm assumes that the source signals are at the
most partially correlated and additive noise is spatially white.

In realistic passive radar scenario one may encounter multiple sources, which
are highly or fully coherent, and an additive noise field corrupting the signal, which is
directional and spatially correlated. The Differential Music Algorithm proposed by
Rajagopal and Rao [2] can be applied to estimate the DOAs of these sources. This
method has been shown to enjoy many advantages over conventional MUSIC. Using
Differential MUSIC DOAs of correlated multipath arrivals could be estimated, but the
source and its specular arrivals cannot be distinguished. M.Zoltowski and Haber
extended the conventional MUSIC algorithm to discriminate between the direct path

A major disadvantage of eigen structure methods is their computational load. In
order to reduce the computational complexity, Q-R decomposition of the difference
matrix was applied in [3]. However this method is also a ‘block’ processing method. In
order to make the DOA estimation algorithms adaptive, Yang and Kaveh [4], proposed
updating techniques. These algorithms have still too high computational complexity and
are not easily mapped into VLSI architectures (like systolic arrays) for real time
applications. Hwang and Wei [5] have proposed a rank revealing QR updating
procedure for DOA tracking. Though this method reduces the computational load
considerably it cannot be applied to the generalized passive Sonar/Radar environment
mentioned above. Rajagopal and Vijay kumar [6] have developed a dynamic and
adaptive DOA estimation algorithm by using a Q-R updating procedure as and when
new data arrive. Passive tracking of targets using angle only information is a classical
navigation problem and has been extensively studied for naval applications [8-13] and
for passive homing missile application [14]. Major efforts associated with the naval
applications have been directed to analyze the observability of the tracking system and
to find the proper filter structures for target state estimation. In all these works, the
bearing information is assumed to be available through measurements made by some
means. No effort has been made to tackle the measurement problem along with the
tracking problem. KVS babu et.al. [15,16] and R. Rajagopal and Vijay Kumar Chakka [6]
addressed this problem, but it is not possible to discriminate sources and their multi
paths and also it is not possible for tracking of maneuverable targets. C.Vijaykumar and
Rajagopal proposed [20] a unified procedure, which combines the DOA estimation and
tracking using Unscented filter based tracker.This algorithm has high computational and
memory requirements. This paper presents a computationally efficient algorithm. This
Unsecented Kalman filter algorithm utilises 50percent less data memory space and 50
percent reduction in the computation without reduction in performance. For adaptive
DOA estimation, the Differential MUSIC algorithm is used. The results obtained are then
applied as input to a Non-Linear State Linear Measurement Unscented Kaiman filter.
This filter avoids the derivation of the Jacobian matrices for linearizing the nonlinear
kinematics model. The Unscented Kalman filter based tracker uses normalized
innovation square (NIS) criterion to detect maneuver and accounts for it by continuous
process noise level adjustment. Simulation results show the good performance of the
filter over the Unscented filter based tracker [20]

This paper is organized into seven sections. Section 2 describes adaptive DOA
Estimation. Section 3 presents Non Linear state Linear measurement for DOA Tracking.
Section 4 presents DOA tracking using Unscented Kaiman filter and section 5 presents
the simulation results and performance comparisons with Unscented filter based tracker
[20] and finally conclusions are presented in section 6.
