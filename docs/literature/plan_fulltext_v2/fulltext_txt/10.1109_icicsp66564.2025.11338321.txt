Abstract—The 
robust 
underwater 
frequency-domain 
direction-of-arrival (DOA) tracking is proposed to develop the 
performance of the DOA tracking in the scenario of uncertain 
environment noise. The time-domain signal received by 
hydrophone array is transform to frequency-domain signal, and 
the frequency-domain measurement model of the bearing angle of 
the target is establish in the frame work of the EKF. Thus, the 
frequency-domain EKF for DOA tracking is proposed. The 
uncertain environment noise in the frequency-domain is easily 
filtered out by extract the frequencies around the frequency of the 
target signal. By utilizing the EKF scheme, the kinematic model of 
the underwater target is introduced to decrease the influence of 
the uncertain measurement noise. By utilizing the frequency-
domain measurement, the uncertain environment noise is easily 
filtered out by extract the frequencies around the frequency of the 
target signal in the frequency domain. The proposed method is 
demonstrated and verified by the simulation, and both of the 
robustness and accuracy are proved. 
Keywords—underwater direction-of-arrival tracking, robust 
tracking, extended Kalman filter, Fourier transform, frequency-
domain tracking 
I. INTRODUCTION 
Estimating the bearing angle of an uncooperative underwater 
target is an important research area in the field of underwater 
acoustic engineering [1]-[3]. Considering the unknown 
underwater environment sometimes causes the uncertain 
measurement noise that will degrade the estimation accuracy of 
the interested target’s bearing angle, robust bearing angle 
estimating technique is necessary and has attracted a number of 
researchers’ attentions [4], [5]. In addition, the passive bearing 
angle estimation techniques can be divided to two categories, 
namely the direction-of -arrival (DOA) estimation that merely 
depends on the measurements from a certain period of time and 
the DOA tracking that depends on both of the target’s 
kinematics and the measurements of the current tracking step. 
Although the DOA estimation has already made a lot of 
milestones in various bearing angle estimation missions [6],[7], 
its ignorance of the target’s kinematics makes the DOA 
estimation techniques always have superior estimating results 
when the target is nearly static and the environment is stable. 
When the target is maneuvering or the measurement noise shifts 
since the underwater environment is uncertain, the DOA 
estimation methods can hardly have satisfied results. Also, in 
order to make reliable and accurate estimations of an 
uncooperative underwater target’s bearing angle, the DOA 
estimation techniques usually need a wide range of observation 
window, which will cost more on-board computational time 
than the recursively DOA tracking techniques. 
Considering the advantages and drawbacks of the DOA 
estimation methods, the DOA tracking techniques have been 
proposed. Instead of merely utilizing the measurements 
information, the DOA tracking methods also consider the 
target’s kinematic model as a prior information. Based on the 
Bayesian estimation principle, the DOA tracking techniques can 
reach higher estimating accuracy and occupy less on-board 
computing or storage resources especially when the underwater 
target is maneuvering [8], [9]. 
Also, in the real scenario of underwater DOA tracking, the 
unknown underwater environment always results in uncertain 
measurement noise, which will degrade the bearing angle 
estimation accuracy or even make the estimating procedure 
diverge. Under this circumstance, the robust DOA tracking 
technique is necessary to guarantee the accuracy and robustness 
of the whole estimation process. To deal with the uncertain 
measurement noise in underwater DOA tracking, the methods 
based on measurement noise covariance matrix (MNCM) 
estimation is proposed to estimate the MNCM in real time of 
tracking and substitute it into the tracking process to reduce the 
tracking error. The existing MNCM-estimation-based methods 
are divided into two categories, the Sage-Husa (SH) methods

and the variational Bayesian (VB) methods. The SH algorithm 
utilizes good estimations from a long tracking period to adjust 
the weight of the poor measurements when the measurement 
noise becomes uncertain. The VB technique assumes the prior 
probability density functions (PDFs) of the covariance matrix of 
the uncertain measurement noise and estimate it along with the 
tracking parameters. SH methods and VB methods are both used 
to carry out robust underwater DOA tracking [10]-[15]. 
However, the SH online estimator depends on good historical 
estimation and measurements, and the VB tracking approach 
depends on the accurate assumption of the PDF of MNCM. 
When the duration of poor measurement is long and the 
assumption of the PDF of MNCM is hard to obtain, the 
performance of SH and VB tracking methods degrades. Thus, 
new method for robust multi-target DOA tracking needs to be 
research. 
To solve the problem of SH and VB tracking methods, we 
proposed the frequency-domain EKF (FD-EKF) for robust DOA 
tracking to perform robust tracking without estimating the 
measurement noise. Firstly, the kinematic model of the 
underwater target is established based on the kinematic 
characteristic of the underwater target. Secondly, the frequency-
domain measurement is established based on the Fourier 
transform technique, which the interested signal is easily 
extracted in frequency-domain. Finally, the frequency-domain 
EKF for robust is proposed based on the established underwater 
target kinematic model and the frequency-domain measurement. 
By extracting the signal at desired frequencies in frequency 
domain and perform frequency-domain EKF tracking technique, 
the effect of the uncertain measurement noise is mostly 
eliminated. Thus, the problem of uncertain measurement noise 
in underwater DOA tracking scenario is solved in a new way. 
The rest of this paper is organized as follows: In Sec. II, the 
problem of underwater multi-target DOA tracking is formulated. 
In Sec. III, the frequency-domain measurement and the FD-EKF 
for DOA tracking is described. In Sec. IV, simulation is made 
and the superior performance of the proposed FD-EKF for 
robust underwater DOA tracking is proved. Finally, conclusions 
are drawn in Sec. V.

V. CONCLUSION 
The robust underwater DOA tracking problem in uncertain 
measurement noise scenario is studied. The FD-EKF for DOA 
tracking is proposed to provide robust tracking in uncertain 
measurement noise scenario. The time-domain signal received 
by hydrophone array to frequency-domain signal and the signal 
at interested frequencies is extracted. Then, the proposed 
method uses extracted signal as the measurement to perform 
robust tracking. Thus, the effect of the uncertain measurement is 
eliminated in a new way different from the existing noise-
estimating methods. 
Compared to the SH tracking method and the VB tracking 
method, which requires accurate assumptions in application 
scenarios, FD-EKF has better universality in underwater target 
DOA tracking scenarios with uncertain measurement noise. 
Since the SH tracking method and the VB tracking method need 
to estimate noise statistics, both of them require to preset the 
hyperparameters of the proposed algorithms before DOA 
tracking. These hyperparameters significantly affect the 
estimation of noise statistics, which resulting in affecting the 
tracking performance. In engineering applications, it is not easy 
to determine these hyperparameters. However, the FD-EKF 
does not need to set the hyperparameters to estimate noise 
statistics, which makes it easier for engineering applications.
