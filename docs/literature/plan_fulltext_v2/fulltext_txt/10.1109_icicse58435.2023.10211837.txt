velocity are solved by optimization algorithm and DOA (Direction

Then the DOA of multiple targets can be distinguished and

I. INTRODUCTION
The detection of target radiated noise and passive DOA 
estimation are always an important question for discussion of 
under water early warning[1]. With the development of 
vibration and noise reduction, radiated noise of underwater 
target reduced year by year, especially in the high frequency 
band. In order to detect long-distance targets, it is generally 
necessary to carry out in the low frequency band. For low 
frequency signals, in order to obtain ideal spatial gain, the 
traditional sound pressure array must have a large physical 
aperture, which is inconvenient or even impossible in 
engineering application. Vector hydrophone can be output 
acoustic pressure and particle velocity at the same point 
synchronously. Single vector hydrophone can detect DOA 
without ambiguity in the whole space[2], which makes it has 
unique advantages in underwater acoustic detection, especially 
on small-scale platforms[3].    
The traditional application methods of DOA estimation 
using single vector hydrophone in engineering include average 
sound intensity meter[4][5], cross spectrum method[6][7] and so 
on. The method of average sound intensity meter is based on 
maximum likelihood estimation of sound energy flow, which 
can suppress the isotropic interference effectively . However, if 
there are multiple targets, it measures the vector sum of the 
sound energy flow of all signals, so it cannot distinguish the 
different targets. Cross spectrum method is a frequency domain 
processing method, which has the ability of distinguishing 
multiple targets. However, it cannot do nothing about targets 
whose characteristic spectra overlap or interfere with each other. 
The literature[8][9] shows that for incoherent targets, when the 
SNR is enough and the line spectrum characteristics are 
uncertain, the number of distinguished targets can reach 7 by 
making full use of the correlation characteristics of acoustic 
pressure and particle velocity in theory. However, The 
experimental results in literature[10] indicate that this method is 
not effective when two targets are close.    
In this paper, a method of multiple targets DOA tracking by 
using single Vector hydrophone based on unite processing in 
time and frequency domain is proposed. Firstly, the relationship 
between acoustic pressure and particle velocity is solved in each 
frequency band, and the high-order correlation equations are 
solved to obtain the DOA of multiple targets. Then the least 
square method is used to fit the DOA trajectory in a period of 
time and establish a dynamic prediction model. Finally, Kalman 
filter is performed and the DOA distribution of targets in each 
frequency band is counted to obtain the pseudo color picture of 
DOA distribution. By using this method, we can not only 
distinguish the DOA trajectory of multiple targets, but also 
observe the azimuth of interference. The accuracy of DOA 
estimation is less than 5°.

Fig. 1. Solution flow of multiple targets DOA tracking 
As shown in Figure 1, firstly, the trend terms of signal are 
removed to eliminate DC and low- frequency interference, and 
then narrow-band filter is carried out. The DOA of each target 
relative to the hydrophone coordinate is obtained by solving 
high-order correlation equations in multiple frequency band. 
The DOA relative to the geodetic coordinate is counted by 
coordinate conversion. The least square method is used to fit 
DOA trajectory of targets, and then the outliers are eliminated 
by Kalman filter. Take the DOA value as integers, count the 
number of DOA  distribution on each angle, normalized process, 
and draw pseudo color picture according to the distribution. 
Extract the highlight in the pseudo color picture and fit the DOA 
trajectory, so as to realize the DOA trajectory of multiple targets 
tracking.

VI. CONCLUSION
A new method of multiple targets DOA tracking with vector 
hydrophone is proposed in this paper. The experiment results 
indicate that the DOA information of multiple targets can be 
solved and tracked stably. The azimuth of interference can also 
be obtained though DOA pseudo color picture. In addition, the 
outliers can be eliminated and the accuracy can be improved by 
the method of fitting DOA data and Kalman filter. In the 
experiment, the accuracy can reach 5°.
