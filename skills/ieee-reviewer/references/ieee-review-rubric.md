# IEEE Review Rubric

## Scoring Scale

Use a 1-5 scale per dimension:
- 5: Excellent
- 4: Strong
- 3: Adequate
- 2: Weak
- 1: Poor

## Dimensions

## 1) Novelty And Significance
- 5: Introduces clearly new ideas with high expected impact.
- 3: Incremental but useful advancement.
- 1: Little to no novelty or relevance.

## 2) Technical Soundness
- 5: Methods and claims are correct, justified, and internally consistent.
- 3: Mostly correct but with moderate gaps.
- 1: Fundamental errors or unsupported core claims.

## 3) Experimental Rigor
- 5: Strong baselines, fair comparisons, ablations, and robust statistics.
- 3: Partial validation with notable omissions.
- 1: Weak or unreliable evidence.

## 4) Clarity And Organization
- 5: Clear writing, strong structure, easy to reproduce logic.
- 3: Understandable but uneven clarity.
- 1: Hard to follow; key details missing.

## 5) Reproducibility
- 5: Full implementation detail, key hyperparameters, and reproducibility artifacts.
- 3: Partial details; reproducibility possible with effort.
- 1: Insufficient details for reproduction.

## Interpreting Overall Recommendation
- Typical accept profile: no dimension below 3 and at least two dimensions at 4+.
- Typical weak reject profile: one critical dimension at 2.
- Typical strong reject profile: one or more critical dimensions at 1, or ethics/correctness blockers.

Treat this as guidance, not rigid law. Final recommendation must reflect paper-specific risk and confidence.
