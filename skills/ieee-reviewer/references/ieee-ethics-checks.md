# IEEE Integrity And Ethics Checks

## Quick Checklist

- Confirm no declared or suspected conflict of interest.
- Flag plagiarism or unattributed reuse signals if visible.
- Check for data leakage between train/validation/test.
- Evaluate fairness risks and subgroup performance gaps if applicable.
- Evaluate privacy and security implications for deployment settings.
- Check whether human/animal subject handling is disclosed when relevant.
- Verify whether limitations and potential misuse are discussed.

## Red Flags That Should Affect Recommendation

- Missing evidence for major safety-sensitive claims.
- Omitted ethics disclosures when clearly needed.
- Inflated claims unsupported by experiments.
- Benchmark manipulation, selective reporting, or unfair baselines.

## Reporting Guidance

When raising ethics concerns:
- State concrete evidence and location in the manuscript.
- Distinguish confirmed issues from concerns needing clarification.
- Provide actionable requests to resolve issues in revision.
