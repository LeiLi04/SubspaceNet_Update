# Deep Learning-Based DOA Estimation: A Comprehensive Survey

**Authors:** Zheng, Shilian; Yang, Zhuang; Shen, Weiguo; Zhang, Luxin; Zhu, Jiawei; Zhao, Zhijin; Yang, Xiaoniu
**Venue:** IEEE Trans. Cognitive Communications and Networking, Vol 10(3), pp 819-835, 2024
**Zotero Key:** ARJXINSD
**Category:** Methods

---

## Research Question
What is the current landscape of deep learning-based DOA estimation, and how can approaches be systematically categorized?

## Core Method
- Comprehensive survey of DL-based DOA estimation
- Taxonomy by architecture type, input representation, and problem formulation
- Reviews CNNs, RNNs, autoencoders, transformers, and hybrid approaches
- Covers classification-based, regression-based, and spectrum-based formulations

## Key Findings
- Hybrid model-data approaches generally outperform pure data-driven methods
- Covariance-based inputs tend to be more robust than raw signal inputs
- Field is moving toward model-based deep learning incorporating physical models
- Key open challenges: generalization, unsupervised learning, real-world deployment

## Limitations
- Survey paper — does not propose new methods
- Rapid pace of field means some recent works may not be covered

## Relevance to Our Research
Provides broad context for positioning SubspaceNet within the DL-DOA landscape. Validates SubspaceNet's hybrid approach as aligned with the field's trajectory. Identified open challenges align with our research gaps.
