# Deep Learning-Enabled One-Bit DoA Estimation

**Authors:** Unknown
**Venue:** arXiv preprint, 2024
**Zotero Key:** AKUBG79I
**Category:** Methods

---

## Research Question
How can deep learning enable DOA estimation from one-bit quantized observations via deep unrolling?

## Core Method
- Deep unrolling approach for DOA estimation with 1-bit data
- Addresses the extreme quantization scenario where only sign information is available
- Formulates DOA as sparse recovery from binary measurements

## Key Findings
- Deep unrolling enables effective DOA estimation despite severe information loss from 1-bit quantization
- Outperforms conventional methods designed for quantized observations

## Limitations
- Preprint — not yet peer-reviewed
- Limited metadata available
- Extreme quantization scenario has limited practical scope

## Relevance to Our Research
Related to TransMUSIC's low-resolution ADC scenario and DeepFPC. Represents the deep unfolding approach to quantized DOA — complementary to SubspaceNet's subspace learning approach.
