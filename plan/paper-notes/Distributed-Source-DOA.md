# Distributed Source DOA Estimation Based on Deep Learning Networks

**Authors:** Tian, Quan; Cai, Ruiyan; Qiu, Gongrun; Luo, Yang
**Venue:** Signal, Image and Video Processing, Vol 18(10), pp 7395-7403, 2024
**Zotero Key:** E2B2UJWJ
**Category:** Methods

---

## Research Question
How can deep learning handle DOA estimation for distributed (spatially spread) sources rather than point sources?

## Core Method
- Deep learning networks for DOA estimation under the distributed source model
- Sources modeled with angular spread rather than point-source assumption
- Estimates both central DOA and angular spread parameters

## Key Findings
- DL effectively estimates DOAs of distributed sources
- Outperforms classical distributed source methods, especially with large angular spread
- Can jointly estimate central DOA and angular spread

## Limitations
- Specific to distributed source model; may not generalize to mixed scenarios
- Published in lower-tier venue
- Primarily simulation-based validation

## Relevance to Our Research
Extends DOA to distributed source model — complementary to SubspaceNet's point-source assumption. Practical scenarios with scattering may require such extensions.
