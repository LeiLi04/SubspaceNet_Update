# Graph Neural Network-Based DOA Estimation Method Exploring Training Data Association

**Authors:** Liu, Ke; Cui, Hengchen; Ma, Junda
**Venue:** Circuits, Systems, and Signal Processing, Vol 44(5), pp 3641-3658, 2025
**Zotero Key:** Q4N3EGCT
**Category:** Applications

---

## Research Question
How does training data composition and association affect GNN-based DOA estimation performance?

## Core Method
- GNN-based DOA estimation with focus on training data strategies
- Explores how different training data compositions impact GNN generalization
- Graph structure captures inter-antenna relationships for DOA estimation

## Key Findings
- Training data association significantly impacts GNN-DOA performance
- Proper data curation improves generalization across scenarios

## Limitations
- Published in mid-tier venue
- Focus on data strategy rather than architectural innovation

## Relevance to Our Research
GNN architecture for DOA — alternative to CNN/Transformer in SubspaceNet. Training data insights applicable to any DL-DOA method.
