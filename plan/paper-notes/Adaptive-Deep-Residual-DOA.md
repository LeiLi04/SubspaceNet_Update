# Adaptive Deep-Residual-Learning Framework for DOA Under Impulsive Noise and Varying Modulation

**Authors:** Yao, Yuanyuan; Li, Mingyang
**Venue:** Electronics Letters, Vol 61(1), 2025
**Zotero Key:** GX44S3QN
**Category:** To-Read

---

## Research Question
How can DOA estimation adapt to unknown signal modulation types under impulsive noise?

## Core Method
- Rotation-invariant (RI) DOA estimation via deep residual learning (DRL)
- RI-ResNN-DAE framework
- Adapts across different signal modulation schemes
- Analyzes correlation between network weights and angular resolution
- Handles impulsive noise (non-Gaussian)

## Key Findings
- Strong adaptability across different signal modulation schemes
- Superior to classical and DL-based methods under impulsive noise
- Network weight analysis provides insights for network scale design
- Rotation-invariant representation improves generalization

## Limitations
- Short letter format limits detail
- Specific to impulsive noise scenarios
- Modulation adaptability may have limits for exotic waveforms

## Relevance to Our Research
Addresses modulation-invariant DOA and impulsive noise robustness — both practical deployment challenges. The adaptation across modulation types connects to our broader research goal of robust, adaptive DOA estimation.
