# Super Resolution DOA Estimation Based on Deep Neural Network

**Authors:** Liu, Wanli
**Venue:** Scientific Reports, Vol 10(1), 2020
**Zotero Key:** WHJGPRFQ
**Category:** Applications

---

## Research Question
Can DNN-based DOA estimation achieve super-resolution while generalizing to random signal numbers and SNR levels?

## Core Method
- Novel DNN framework for super-resolution DOA estimation
- Achieves generalization to random signal number and varying SNR
- Pure data-driven approach without explicit subspace decomposition
- Published in Nature journal family (Scientific Reports)

## Key Findings
- Higher resolution and better generalization than previous DNN-DOA works
- Handles unknown signal numbers and varying SNR
- State-of-the-art performance at time of publication

## Limitations
- Pure data-driven — lacks interpretability of model-based approaches
- May not generalize to array configurations not seen during training
- Earlier work (2020) — surpassed by hybrid approaches like SubspaceNet

## Relevance to Our Research
Purely data-driven baseline representing the end-to-end approach that SubspaceNet improves upon. Demonstrates limitations of black-box DL-DOA that motivate hybrid model-based approaches.
